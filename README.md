# Financial Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A business-focused sales data analysis project using SQL, statistics, and Power BI to identify sales and profitability patterns across products, segments, countries, and months.

The project demonstrates how a Business Analyst can use data to answer business questions, identify trends, and communicate insights to support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Leadership needs a clearer view of sales and profit performance across different products, customer segments, countries, and time periods.

Without structured analysis, it can be difficult to identify:

- High-performing products
- High-performing customer segments
- Strong and weak countries
- Monthly sales patterns
- Differences between sales and profitability

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze sales data using SQL

## 📊 Key Findings

### Average vs Median Sale Value

The average sale value was approximately **₹169,609**, while the median was approximately **₹35,540**.

The difference suggests that a smaller number of large transactions influence the average, making the median useful for understanding a more typical transaction value.

---

### Top Product by Sales

**Paseo** was identified as the top product by total sales, with approximately **₹33.0M** in sales.

---

### Segment Performance

The **Government** segment had the highest sales and profit among the analyzed segments:

- Sales: approximately **₹52.5M**
- Profit: approximately **₹11.4M**

---

### Country Performance

The **USA** had the highest sales among the analyzed countries, with approximately **₹25.0M** in sales.

---

### Monthly Performance

**October** had the highest monthly sales, at approximately **₹21.7M**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Investigate the factors contributing to the strong profitability of the Government segment.

2. Monitor high-performing products such as Paseo and investigate the factors contributing to their sales performance.

3. Investigate the reasons behind October's strong sales performance and determine whether successful factors can be applied to other periods.

4. Review country-level sales and profitability together rather than relying only on sales volume.

5. Higher discount levels appear to be associated with weaker profitability in the analyzed data and should be investigated further before making pricing or discounting decisions.

> Note: These recommendations are based on the analyzed dataset and should be validated with additional business and operational data before implementation.
